# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0). The short form is below; the linked text is authoritative where they differ.

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in FENGARDE a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith, especially from first-time contributors.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public.
The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, for behavior they deem inappropriate.

**To report a problem privately:** open a private report via GitHub (Security -> Report a vulnerability) and
title it "Conduct report"; it reaches the maintainer only. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's
privacy is respected.

Enforcement follows the Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.
